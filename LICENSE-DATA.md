## Third-party content is NOT covered

CC BY 4.0 applies only to the author's own classification, aggregation and writing. It does **not**
relicense content created by others, which remains the property of its authors and is subject to the
terms of the originating platforms:

- `data/hn/raw/`, `data/hn/v1/`, `data/classified/input/` — comments from [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/) (via the Algolia HN API)
- `data/community/raw/` — comments from [Lobste.rs](https://lobste.rs/) and [Tildes](https://tildes.net/)
- short verbatim quotes (≤160 chars) inside the derived files, included for verification
- `data/readwise/` metadata and summaries of articles saved in the author's Readwise Reader library
- `data/surveys/surveys_2026.md` — figures cited from Stack Overflow, JetBrains, The Pragmatic Engineer and GitHub, with links to the sources

Full texts of third-party articles are deliberately **not** included (see `.gitignore`).
If you are a quoted author and want a comment or quote removed, please open an issue.
